Delay travels with the train

A train that is 12 minutes late at a station may have lost only one minute there. It brought the other eleven with it. So if you want to learn how weather causes delay, total delay is a poor target: it mixes the weather at this station with everything that happened earlier on the line.

FI-TW joins Finnish train-running records (Digitraffic) with nearby weather observations (Finnish Meteorological Institute) for 2018–2024. Its baseline model predicts the station-specific delay with the propagated part removed. That is the orange part in the essay above.
