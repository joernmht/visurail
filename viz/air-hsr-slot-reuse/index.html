The slot is the catch

Moving short feeder flights onto high-speed rail looks like a clear climate gain. At a congested hub, though, every feeder flight holds a runway slot. Once the flight moves to rail, the slot is free, and airlines can fill it again, often with a longer and more profitable route.

Dobruszkes studies Frankfurt Airport ex post, over a period in which airport capacity stayed stable. The paper finds that air/HSR integration did not reduce aviation's climate impact there, because freed slots were reused. Integration is not a climate fix on its own; its effect depends on what happens to the slots.
